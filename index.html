About

I've been working with my hands for more than 10 years. On a farm packing line I ran a robotic palletizer and changed its programs remotely over VNC. I fix phones, PCs and game consoles, and today I work as a field service technician repairing food service equipment, with some HVAC and refrigeration work.

All of it comes down to troubleshooting: figuring out why something broke and getting it running again. Now I'm putting that into code, learning to program and mod games. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

I also use AI in my projects, and I'm learning to use it the right way: as a tool that helps me learn and build, not something that does the thinking for me. I want to understand what I'm making, not just “vibe code” it.
